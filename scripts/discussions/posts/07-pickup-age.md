# "Waiting N hours, nobody has looked" in the review queue
category: Ideas

Today: rows show what blocks a PR but not how long it has been waiting on the current turn, and "no reviewers" covers both "nobody requested" and "a team was requested and nobody answered".

The review queue would show how long a PR has waited for its first review and sort by it, and would tell apart "no reviewer requested" from "a team was requested and nobody responded".

Vote with 👍. Comment if you would rather see this on your own PRs, on the review queue, or both.
