# Whose move is it? Ownership edge cases
category: Ideas

Today: the Note follows the shell prototype rules (CI, reviews, unresolved threads, conflicts, missing reviewers) and does not look at who spoke last in a thread or route a conflicted teammate PR back to its author.

Rules the board would apply: a conflicted teammate PR waits on its author, not on you; an outdated thread brings the PR back to "needs you"; your reply clears your turn and the author replying returns it; a re-run check that passed clears "fix CI".

Vote with 👍. Comment with a case where PR Marmot (or GitHub) told you it was your move when it was not.
