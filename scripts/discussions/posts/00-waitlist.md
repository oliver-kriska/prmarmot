# iPad app + Cloud: notify me
category: Announcements
pin: true

PR Marmot on the desktop (macOS + Linux) is free and open source, and stays that way.

Two things are on the roadmap above it, and both only get built if enough people want them:

- **PR Marmot for iPad and iPhone** — the same board and the same "what do you do next" Note, native, no server, signed in with your GitHub account. Paid, one-time.
- **PR Marmot Cloud** — a watcher that runs while your laptop is closed and pings your phone when a watched PR is ready for you: CI green, review in, conflict, stack unblocked. Subscription.

**To get notified, react with 👍 on this post or leave a comment.** That is the whole waitlist. No email form, no third-party service: the count of people here is the signal that decides whether these ship (the exact gate is in [ROADMAP.md](https://github.com/oliver-kriska/prmarmot/blob/main/ROADMAP.md)).

If you want a specific feature on the desktop app instead, vote in [Ideas](https://github.com/oliver-kriska/prmarmot/discussions/categories/ideas) or open a new one there.
