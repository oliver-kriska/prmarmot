# Show what changed since your last review, not just that something changed
category: Ideas

Today: the review queue marks a PR as changed since you last looked at it in the app, and the Note says "new commits since your review", but not how many or what.

When a PR comes back for re-review, the app would say "3 commits, 4 files, 1 thread reopened since you approved", anchored to the commit you actually reviewed, not to the clock. GitHub only shows an unread dot.

Vote with 👍 if this would save you a re-read. Comment with what you would want listed: commits, files, threads, CI runs.
